Source: New Zealand Ministry of Education, Tāhūrangi. Curriculum materials are available under CC BY-NC 4.0, subject to the source copyright notice. The Chinese teaching map is an adaptation; lessons and diagrams are original teaching materials. This project is not endorsed by the Ministry.

This collection covers Year 3–8 objectives through picture lessons, practice, short checks, later review and practical activities. Year 1–2 are outside this collection. Objective mapping records where an idea is taught; short online checks cannot establish whole-year mastery or verify measuring, constructing, investigating and experimenting in the real world.
